Choose Your Setup

On-Grid, Off-Grid & Hybrid Solar

Not every roof needs the same system. Here's a quick look at the three setups we design and install.

On-Grid Solar

Connected straight to the utility grid with no batteries — the most affordable option. Extra power is exported back to the grid, though the system shuts off during a power cut for safety.

Off-Grid Solar

A fully independent system built around battery storage, for sites with no grid access. All power is generated, stored and used on site — ideal for remote homes and estates.

Hybrid Solar

Grid connection plus battery backup in one system. Solar powers the home first, batteries take over during outages, and the grid tops up when needed — steady power and long-term savings.
